📜 Privacy Policy

We value your privacy. This app is designed with a user-first approach and does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

📌 What We Collect

The app does not require any login, registration, or personal data from users. There are no background trackers, analytics, or data collection tools. You can use the app completely offline once it’s downloaded.

📁 Permissions

The app requests Storage Access permission only to allow saving ebook files directly to your device. This enables offline reading of downloaded materials. No other permissions are requested or used.

We do not sell, license, or host any content. The app simply organizes and displays ebooks that are freely and legally available online.
